1. Corporate Profile, Business Model & Competitive Moat

PT Mayora Indah Tbk (IDX: MYOR) stands as Indonesia's premier branded consumer food & beverage conglomerate. Operating across two core analytical reporting segments—Packaged Food Processing (59.0% of revenue: Roma, Beng-Beng, Choki-Choki, Kopiko Candy) and Beverages Processing (41.0% of revenue: Torabika, Energen, Kopiko Lucky Day, allied with Teh Pucuk Harum & Le Minerale).

The Global Export Moat: Unlike domestic-focused Indonesian consumer peers (e.g., Unilever Indonesia whose exports represent <6%), Mayora derives over 44% of total revenue from international markets spanning >100 countries. In the Philippines, Kopiko commands a market-leading >45% share in instant coffee mixes. This unique geographic diversification acts as a natural FX hedge during IDR volatility and unlocks global economies of scale across manufacturing hubs in Cikupa, Balaraja, and Pasuruan.

3. Supply Chain Architecture & Inbisco Route-to-Market Moat

Mayora's commercial dominance in Indonesia rests on an integrated downstream supply chain anchored by PT Inbisco Niagatama Semesta (exclusive nationwide distributor). Inbisco operates a two-tiered fulfillment model:

  • General Trade (GT) Dominance: Direct access to >1.5 million traditional warungs and mom-and-pop stores via micro-distribution sub-agents, utilizing affordable single-serve price points (Rp 1,000 – Rp 2,000 sachets for Energen, Torabika Gilus, and Choki-Choki).
  • Modern Trade (MT) Efficiency: Direct store delivery (DSD) and centralized vendor-managed replenishment to Indomaret, Alfamart, and supermarket chains with an industry-leading OTIF service level exceeding 96.5%.
  • Working Capital Cycle: Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC) managed at 88 days (DIO: 64 days, DSO: 58 days, DPO: 34 days), maintaining strong operating cash flows (>Rp 4.0T annually) that fully self-fund capex expansion.

5. Commodity Sensitivity Analysis & Risk Management

Raw materials comprise 82.5% of total COGS. The primary commodity exposures include Sugar (22.0%), Wheat Flour (20.5%), and Robusta Coffee (18.5%). A ±10% unexpected inflation shock across these 3 commodities impacts Mayora's gross profit by approximately Rp 1.76 Triliun (±165 bps on Gross Margin). Mayora mitigates this risk through:

✓ Strategic Mitigations & Outlook (2026 - 2027):
  • Forward Contracting & Strategic Inventory Buffers: 3 to 6-month forward procurement contracts on CBOT wheat futures and domestic coffee harvests to smooth out spot volatility.
  • Pack-Size & Grammage Optimization: Implementing "invisible shrinkflation" (adjusting biscuit count or grammage per sachet by 2–4%) during extreme commodity peaks to protect price-elastic entry points (Rp 1,000/pack).
  • Export Pricing Elasticity: Higher willingness to pay in international markets (Philippines, Middle East) allows passing through ingredient cost inflation more rapidly than in domestic GT channels.
  • Beverage Synergy: Rapid expansion of RTD beverages (Kopiko Lucky Day) delivers superior gross margins (32–35%) compared to traditional dry groceries.
